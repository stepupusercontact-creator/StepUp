Introduction

Welcome to StepUp (referred to as "StepUp", "we", "our", or "us"). StepUp is a dedicated school management software platform developed and operated by the StepUp Team as an independent software initiative.

StepUp provides educational institutions with a secure, centralized platform to record, manage, and communicate attendance, evaluations, grades, homework assignments, academic calendars, school announcements, and compliance records. StepUp replaces unorganized messaging groups, fragmented spreadsheets, and physical registers, providing structured and transparent communication channels between school leadership, teachers, parents, and students.

This Privacy Policy sets out how personal data is collected, processed, protected, and retained when educational institutions, school administrators, teachers, parents, and students interact with our web applications and digital interfaces. This policy has been prepared in full alignment with the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA 2023) of India and its implementing rules.

1 Legal Architecture: Data Fiduciary vs. Data Processor

In compliance with the statutory definitions under the DPDPA 2023, the legal relationship and responsibilities between StepUp and the educational institution are defined as follows:

A. The School as the Data Fiduciary

The school (the private or autonomous educational institution entering into an agreement with StepUp) is the Data Fiduciary. The school determines the purpose, necessity, and scope of student, parent, and teacher data onboarded, and retains legal ownership and authority over all institutional and student records.

B. StepUp as the Data Processor

StepUp acts strictly as a Data Processor processing school-managed personal data solely on behalf of, and in accordance with the documented instructions of, the licensed school. StepUp does not claim independent ownership of student or academic records, nor does it sell, commercialize, or independently mine school data.

C. Direct Operational Records

StepUp acts as an independent Data Fiduciary strictly with respect to basic account administration, direct technical support communication with school leadership, billing records, and audit logs maintained for cybersecurity integrity.

2 Categories of Personal Data Processed

StepUp collects and processes only the minimal data strictly required to facilitate school operations and academic tracking:

Student Data Full name, assigned grade/class, section, roll number/student ID, daily attendance entries, homework submissions, teacher grading/evaluations, and academic calendar activity.
Teacher Data Full name, institutional email address, assigned classes and subjects, encrypted login credentials, attendance marking timestamps, and homework dispatch records.
Parent & Guardian Data Full name, verified mobile phone number, email address, relationship to the enrolled student, and login credentials/OTPs.
School Admin Data Full name, administrative designation, login credentials, dashboard interactions, and institutional audit trail records.
Technical Logs Device and browser variety, IP address, login timestamps, application health diagnostics, and error stack traces.

3 Legitimate Purposes of Processing

Personal data is processed exclusively for explicit educational and administrative purposes:

  • Enabling single-entry workflows for teachers to publish attendance, grades, announcements, and homework once.
  • Providing instant, personalized visibility of homework, due dates, attendance, and school events to parents and students.
  • Furnishing school administrators with live visibility into class-by-class attendance, teacher participation, homework completion, and compliance files.
  • Verifying user identities, authenticating sessions, and maintaining access security.
  • Assisting the school in fulfilling statutory reporting required by affiliated educational boards or municipal directorates.

4. Special Safeguards for Children’s Personal Data (Section 9, DPDPA 2023)

As StepUp is built for primary and secondary K-12 education, the overwhelming majority of students are minors under 18 years of age. We enforce strict compliance with Section 9 of the DPDPA 2023:

A. Verifiable Parental Consent

StepUp processes personal data of minor students only upon verifiable consent of the student's parent or lawful guardian. The school, as Data Fiduciary, warrants that it obtains verifiable consent during admission/onboarding, or utilizes StepUp's verified parent-authentication workflow prior to student account activation.

B. Absolute Prohibition on Behavioral Tracking & Profiling

StepUp does not engage in behavioral tracking, browsing habit analysis, psychographic profiling, or commercial classification of children under any circumstances.

C. Absolute Prohibition on Targeted Advertising

StepUp contains zero third-party advertising. We do not display targeted, behavioral, contextual, or sponsored commercial advertisements to children or their guardians.

D. Child Well-Being Commitment

StepUp deploys no algorithmic mechanics, notifications, or automated processes designed or likely to cause any detrimental effect on the physical or psychological well-being of a child.

5 Role-Based Access Control & Visibility

Data access within StepUp is compartmentalized strictly across four distinct user roles:

Role Permitted Data Visibility & Boundaries
Admins Compliance files, school-wide attendance, master registers, and live dashboards reflecting homework completion and teacher updates across all sections.
Teachers Strictly scoped to their assigned classes and subjects (attendance, evaluations, homework dispatches, and class notices). No visibility into non-assigned classes.
Parents Restricted solely to their own enrolled child's homework, upcoming deadlines, attendance logs, and school notices. Peer records are completely inaccessible.
Students Restricted solely to their own homework tasks, submission deadlines, individual attendance status, and the institutional academic calendar.

7 Data Retention and Systematic Erasure

StepUp retains personal data only during the active tenure of the school's licensing contract, and as instructed by the school:

  • Annual Promotion: At the close of each academic session, the school administration instructs which student files are promoted, archived, or marked for deletion.
  • Contract Termination: Upon termination of the school's agreement, StepUp provides a 30-day export window allowing the school to retrieve all institutional records in structured standard formats (CSV/JSON).
  • Permanent Erasure: Within 60 days after the export window concludes, StepUp permanently purges, overwrites, or anonymizes all school-managed records from production databases and backups, retaining only records mandated by applicable Indian tax, corporate, or audit laws.

8 Information Security Safeguards

StepUp implements technical and organizational measures to ensure data integrity and confidentiality:

Cryptographic Standards TLS 1.3 protocol for all data in transit; AES-256 bit encryption for databases and disk backups.
Credential Security Passwords hashed using salted one-way hashing algorithms (bcrypt/Argon2); passwords are never stored in readable format.
Tenant Isolation Logical separation of school databases to prevent cross-institutional data bleed or accidental exposure.
Vulnerability Management Periodic static code reviews, access audit reviews, and security patches deployed to system dependencies.

9 72-Hour Personal Data Breach Protocol

In accordance with statutory mandates under the DPDPA 2023:

Mandatory Notification Window: In the event of a verified personal data breach affecting personal data held by StepUp, StepUp will immediately isolate the incident and notify the affected School Data Fiduciary and the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) without undue delay and within 72 hours of confirming the breach.

Notice Specifics: The breach notification will describe the nature of the breach, the classes of records impacted, estimated numbers of affected Data Principals, probable consequences, containment actions taken, and recommended remedial measures.

10 Rights of Data Principals

Data Principals (parents, adult students, teachers, and staff) are endowed with explicit rights under Chapter III of the DPDPA 2023:

1. Right to Access Information

Right to obtain a summary of personal data being processed and a list of sub-processors with whom data has been shared.

2. Right to Correction & Erasure

Right to request correction of inaccurate or outdated details, and erasure of records no longer necessary for educational operations.

3. Right of Grievance Redressal

Right to have grievances resolved through the school's grievance cell or StepUp's designated Grievance Officer.

4. Right to Nominate

Right to nominate an individual who shall exercise statutory rights on behalf of the Data Principal in the event of death or incapacity.

* Note: Because student and academic evaluations are governed by the school as Data Fiduciary, parents and teachers should primarily route access, correction, and deletion requests through their school administration.
